## Notes

- Searched Pexels, Unsplash and Wikimedia Commons. Wikimedia had no suitable landscape photo under CC0 / public domain / CC BY for these three subjects, so no CC BY file is used and no attribution is mandatory.
- Pixabay was not searched.
- No free photo was found of a modern Volkswagen on a lift inside a workshop; the three VW files are the closest matches (two engine-bay service shots, one classic Beetle in a workshop).
- Candidates looked at and dropped: US tow trucks with company branding and phone numbers, photos with readable foreign plates (Hong Kong, Mexico, Netherlands), a crash scene, cars in showrooms or museums, and Unsplash+ (paid) photos.
