## Contract with the stage script (assets/js/main.js)

- **Section root:** `<section class="entry s-<slug>" id="<slug>" data-lane="left|right" aria-labelledby="...">` containing one `<div class="entry__inner">` (the 12-col measure grid; you may change its grid/layout inside `.s-<slug> .entry__inner`). The road runs down the section in its lane from `top + 36px` to `bottom − 36px`; between sections it crosses horizontally around the boundary with corner radius ~144px. Keep text out of the lane and keep full-width coloured boards (navy) out of the lane and out of the crossing zone (first/last ~130px of the section on the side where the road comes from/goes to) — the car is drawn under every z-index:2 element, so a board over the road hides the car.
- **Lane geometry** (what `harness.html` draws): wide screens: lane centre `x = max(26, innerLeft × 0.8)` from the edge (≈77px at 1440), road width ≈150px at 1440 (≈2–152px). Narrow (<900): lane centre 36px from the edge, road ≈54px wide. Your mock assumed ≈150px centre; re-check placement against the real lane.
- **Layers:** paper/backgrounds (z auto) < road + 3D car (z 1) < content with `position: relative; z-index: 2` (text, plates, boards). Anything the car should drive *over* (e.g. a background photo) must stay without z-index; anything the car passes *under* gets z 2.
- **Do not** use the class `entry__text` (the script snaps it to a ruling and expects an h2).
- **Stamps:** any element with `data-stamp` gets the class `is-stamped` when the car draws level with it (reduced motion: at load). Use it for ticks/stamps that press in as the car passes.
- **hero:** `<header class="banner s-hero" id="icerik">` with three `<span class="park" data-park="volvo|mercedes|vw"></span>` positioned (absolute, %) where each car stands; the road starts at the Volvo's spot. The 3D cars render at about 95px per metre at 1440 (XC60 ≈ 450px long, parked three-quarter view, nose left); on narrow screens ≈ 0.11×W px per metre.
- **marka:** a `<div class="bay" data-bay></div>` where the turntable stands (wide screens; hidden <900 by the base). The stage script draws the podium itself into the road layer, sized to the bay's width, sitting at the bay's foot; the road runs vertically through the bay's centre x. Delete your own podium drawing. The brand picker must be `<fieldset data-brands>` with `<input type="radio" name="marka" value="volvo|mercedes|vw">` (the stage swaps the car to the picked brand).
- **miras:** the section root carries `data-truck` (the car turns into the truck on the crossing onto this page; the paper ages to cream from here).
- **final:** root `<section class="finale s-final" id="iletisim" data-truck-lane="left" data-door="L R T B">` (door fractions of the photo, e.g. `0.156 0.474 0.374 0.86`), containing `<div class="finale__inner">`, a `.store` with `<img class="store__photo">` and `<img class="store__walls" aria-hidden="true" alt="">` (same image; set your clip-path on `.s-final .store__walls`), and the contact block as `<address class="dealer">`.
