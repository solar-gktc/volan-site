# hero2 · v1 · Kapak ve levhalar (cover and plates)

- **Idea:** the hero is the navy cover board of the service booklet. The slide text is on the left. On the right is a small stack of tipped-in plates; a switch lifts the top plate off and the next one settles on top. Below the board is a strip of pale stock (the forecourt) that never changes, and the cars park there. There is no logo in the hero: the menu logo is the only one.
- **Layout (1440×900):** the board fills the hero minus the strip (strip `clamp(224px, 34% of hero, 300px)`, about 282px here). Its gold double foil rule drops from the menu's own foil rule and closes at the bottom. Text uses columns 1–6: the headline is Besley 64/68 in gold ink, the subline Besley 20/30, then the stamp buttons. The stack uses columns 8–12, is 500px wide and sits high, so a parked Mercedes roof clears it. The index tabs hang from the board's lower edge onto the paper, under the text.
- **Slides and images:** (1) h1 "Aracınız bizim sorumluluğumuzda" with the photo plate `/gc-volan-1/assets/img/hakkimizda-xc90-lift.webp`. (2) h2 "Uzun yoldan önce ücretsiz kontrol". Its plate is a perforated coupon card: "Ücretsiz / yol öncesi kontrol", the four scope items ticked in ink, a double-ruled "Son gün 15.01.2027" box, a typed "örnek kampanya" note, and `/gc-volan-1/assets/img/anasayfa/kampanya-jant-volvo.webp` breaking out of a dashed die-cut circle past the card's edge. (3) h2 "1954’ten beri aynı işin başındayız" with `/general-assets/gorseller/arsiv-restore/arsiv-01-atolye-tezgah-matkap-sb-restore-1600.webp`, with the violet 1954 seal pressed on its corner. The plates under the top one show only their border and picture edge (no seal, no wheel). Photo plates carry no captions (site-wide rule); the alt text stays.
- **Switching:** the slides auto-advance every 6.5 s. The gold bar inside the current tab fills during that time, and the next slide starts when the fill ends. Hover or focus anywhere in the hero holds the bar. The "Durdur/Oynat" tab stops and restarts the rotation. The tabs follow the ARIA tablist pattern (arrow keys, Home, End), and the panels that are not shown are `inert`. With `prefers-reduced-motion` there is no auto-advance, no pause tab, and no motion. `?slide=N` shows slide N and stops on it.
- **Cars:** the `.park` spans sit inside the forecourt strip, in % of the strip. Wide: Volvo 52%/54%, Mercedes 77%/36%, VW 87%/76%. Narrow: Volvo 42%/60%, Mercedes 73%/30%, VW 76%/80%. The road starts under the Volvo, runs left and turns down into the left lane (77px wide, 36px narrow). The strip has no z-index and never changes, so the cars never stand on a picture that switches. A car roof may overlap the board's lower edge, which reads as a car parked in front of the cover. Only the text, the plates and the tabs are z 2.
- **Mobile (390):** the board stacks the text, then the plate stack at full width (the coupon scales down). The tabs hang below the board, and a 250px forecourt follows with the three cars and the road.
- **Needs real content / checks:** the campaign terms and date are samples, marked "örnek". Tune the park spots once the real 3D cars render. Check that the Mercedes roof clears the plate stack and the Volvo clears the tabs, especially on short screens (1280×720). The h1 is in slide 1, so while slide 2 or 3 is shown the h1 is hidden (inert). An alternative is to keep a visually hidden page h1 and make all three slide titles h2.
- Mock only: the menu stand-in (static), the road SVG drawn by script, and the red dashed car footprints.
