# hero2 · v2 — Etiketli baskı (full-bleed print with a label) · chosen

- **Idea:** each slide is a large print that bleeds off the top and both sides and stops above a strip of bare paper. A typed paper card is stapled onto the print and carries the words. There is no logo inside the hero and no dark overlay.
- **Layout (1440):** the print is the hero height minus a 220px paper strip, and it casts the plate shadow onto the strip. The card (548px, tilted per slide, two staples, navy double rule) sits on the left of the print. All three card bodies share one grid cell, so the card never changes size. There is a square double-ruled arrow button (56px, paper with a navy double rule) at each side edge, centred vertically on the print. Three square dots sit centred at the foot of the print; the current one turns into a filled diamond.
- **Slides:** (1) the whole facade at blue hour (`bina-mavi-saat-hero-21x9`; phones: `bina-mavi-saat-2560` cropped 16:10). The card covers Oto Kuaför; the entrance and the sign stay visible. (2) "Markanızı bilen ustalar": two mechanics under an XC90 (`Photos-1-001/0Y7B2311.JPG`). The card covers the left mechanic; the one facing the camera stays visible. (3) The campaign as a navy ad: "Ücretsiz" in big light-gold type, the wheel render with its line drawing behind it in gold, and a ticked list of what is checked. The card holds "Yol öncesi kontrol", the terms, the stamped "Son gün 15.01.2027" and the "örnek kampanya" note. There is a visually hidden h1 for the page; the slide titles are h2, and hidden slides are inert and aria-hidden.
- **Switching:** the new print is pulled down over the old one (a clip-path wipe, 1.15s, with a paper edge), and the card text fades over. Auto-advance runs every 7s. It pauses while the pointer is over the hero, while focus is inside it, or while the hero is off screen. It stops for good once an arrow or a dot is used. Left/right keys work on the arrows and dots. With reduced motion there is no auto-advance and no motion. Without JS, slide 1 shows and the controls stay hidden. `?slide=N` in the mock clicks dot N.
- **Cars:** the park spots are on the paper strip (VW 16%/48%, Volvo 37%/78%, Mercedes 70%/46% of the strip), never on a photo, so they look right on every slide. The cars draw over the print's foot. The road starts under the Volvo and goes to the left lane below the hero. Phones: a 200px strip under the card (VW 25/40, Volvo 52/68, Mercedes 73/36).
- **Mobile:** the print is 16:10 with arrows (48px) at the edges and dots at its foot. The card overlaps the print by 14px. The forecourt follows.
- **Files:** the mock `v2.html` is built from `hero/fragment.html` and links the real `assets/css/anasayfa/hero.css` and `assets/js/anasayfa/hero.js`.
