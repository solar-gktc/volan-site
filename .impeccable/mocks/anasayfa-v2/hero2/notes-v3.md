# Hero v3: Açık kitapçık (open booklet spread)

- **Idea:** the service booklet lies open on the stock; each slide is a spread (words on the left page, picture on the right page), and the cars stand on the stock in front of the booklet.
- **Layout (1440):** navy cover board with a foil hairline, two hairline sheet edges, paper pages, a fold drawn as one rule plus spaced hatching, and the pages dipping into the fold at the top and bottom edge (no gradients). Booklet = `--hero-h − 260px` tall, 24px under the menu, on the 1248 measure. No logo in the hero.
- **Slides:** (1) h1 "Aracınız bizim sorumluluğumuzda" + Randevu al / Ara / WhatsApp; photo plate with navy mounting corners: `/gc-volan-1/assets/img/hakkimizda-xc90-lift.webp`. (2) "Ücretsiz yol öncesi kontrol" + Randevu al / Ara; the right page is a printed coupon: a dashed cut line and scissors in one 1.5px stroke, a double frame, the wheel `/gc-volan-1/assets/img/anasayfa/kampanya-jant-volvo.webp`, a Kapsam tick list, a "Son gün 15.01.2027" box, marked "örnek". (3) "1954’ten beri aynı özenle" + Randevu al / Hikâyemiz; the archive print `/general-assets/gorseller/arsiv-restore/arsiv-13-volvo-servis-liftte-arac-restore-1600.webp` (no caption), the 1954 seal, and a typed ledger of facts (1954, 2017, 25, 3).
- **Switching:** a leaf turns over the fold (WAAPI `perspective() rotateY`, 1.1s, with a flat 7% navy tint; the faces are clones of the real pages). Going back turns the leaf the other way. Controls: three ribbon bookmarks under the booklet (the current one is navy and longer; a gold line on it shows the 6.5s timer), page numbers with dog-ears in the top outer corners (previous / next), and a typed Durdur/Oynat button. Pauses on hover and on focus. Reduced motion: no auto-advance, no turn (instant swap). `?slide=N` shows slide N statically.
- **Cars:** Volvo 50% / 92% (body overlaps only the empty bottom margin of the pages; the road starts under it), Mercedes 79% / 85%, VW 89.5% / 94% (half scale, on the stock right of the Volvo). No car stands on a picture, so nothing looks wrong when the slides change. The page text, plates and coupon sit at z 2 and stay clear of the dotted body boxes. While a leaf turns (z 3), it can cover a car roof at the page foot for about 1s.
- **Mobile (<900):** the booklet stands upright: the text page above, the picture page below (the fold becomes a horizontal rule; the "next" dog-ear moves to the bottom-right corner). The swap is a short fade and slide, not a 3D turn. The cars park on the stock under the ribbons (`top: calc(100% − …px)`), and the road goes to the 36px lane. The hero is taller than the screen (about 1470px).
- **Needs real content:** campaign terms and date (sample), and a check of the car footprints with the real stage. They are estimated from 95px/m, the parked yaw and half scale for Mercedes/VW; I did not verify them against the WebGL render. Mock-only: `--vh0` (the hero height is fixed at load for screenshots), the road drawn by the script, the footprint SVGs, and the `min-height: 0` override of menu.css's `.banner` rule.
