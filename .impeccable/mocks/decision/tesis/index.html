Tesisimiz: üç eskiz

The model is a static render in these sketches; on the real page it can be rotated, zoomed and clicked. The unit pins are placed roughly. Photos: client photos (Mekanik, Elektrik, Oto Kuaför); Ofis, Bekleme Salonu and Kaporta use model views because there are no photos of them.

A · Cephe açılır (the facade opens)

The first screen is the real facade photo, full width. When you scroll, the photo turns into the 3D model from the same angle, and the model then turns into the cutaway. Clicking a unit opens its card beside the model, joined to the unit by a line. A unit bar sits along the bottom. open full size

B · Kapak ve sahne (cover and stage)

One screen. Left: navy cover with the title, the facade photo as a tipped-in plate, and Randevu al / Ara. Right: the model. All units are always visible around the model as small tabs with a photo; clicking one (or the model) opens its full card in place. Mekanik opens two cards side by side. View buttons: Kesit, Dış cephe, Katları ayır. open full size

C · Kat kat (floor by floor)

The model is shown with its three floors pulled apart. The cards sit at the height of their floor: Bekleme Salonu beside the upper floor, Mekanik / Elektrik / Ofis beside the main floor, Kaporta and Boya beside the lower floor. The facade photo is a plate on the left: "from outside". open full size
