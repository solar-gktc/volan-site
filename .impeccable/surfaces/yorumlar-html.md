# Yorumlar taslağı (yorumlar.html)

## Scope and mode
Draft of the customer reviews page (menu: Hakkımızda → Multimedya → Yorumlar). Mode: Persuade. Turkish only. Inside the Service Booklet world (DESIGN.md); own composition. Not linked from the menu until approved.

## Audience, job, action
A post-warranty Volvo owner reads what other owners wrote, sees where each comment was published, then contacts Volan. Primary action: Randevu al (WhatsApp); Ara and Yol tarifi beside it.

## Proof and content
- Ten reviews, read verbatim from https://www.volan.com.tr/yorumlar on 2026-11-16 (four dated 24-07-2025, six dated 19-07-2023; the dates are the site's publishing timestamps). Eight of them also stand under the homepage heading "Google Yorumları"; two (Durmuş Koçak, Sevim türkçelik) are on /yorumlar only and are labelled "Müşteri yorumu". The source labels are assigned by this page, not published by the site; the footnote under the slips says how each was assigned.
- No star ratings, no average, no Google review count: the live site shows none. They are blank fields on the page.
- One review says "Türkiye'deki ilk Volvo yetkili servisi"; it stays inside the quote with a typed note that it is the customer's statement.
- Structures considered, by resonance: 1 guest-book ruled pages; 2 year leaves on aged stock; 3 one lead quote over a list; 4 grouped by the job done, with index tabs; 5 the homepage postcard deck, enlarged; 6 quotes beside a sticky contact block; 7 a card-index box of source slips.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The reviews are a card-index box: each review is one record slip whose printed head states source, publishing date and slip number before the words begin. It refuses the star-rating carousel and the grid of equal testimonial cards with avatars.

OWN-WORLD: The Service Booklet unchanged: card stock, navy rules, plate-white paper objects with the plate shadow, Archivo Narrow form labels over Courier Prime typed values, Besley for the customer's words, stamp-violet rubber stamp, square index tabs, double-ruled stamp-box buttons.

STORY: The visitor sees one real review at reading size with its source typed above it, learns that the texts are unedited, reads the other nine by year, finds a blank slip that invites their own review, and reaches the dealer block.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440×900): columns 1–5: H1 "Yorumlar" (navy, about 62px), a two-sentence lead, a three-row typed record (slip count, sources, years), then Randevu al (primary), Ara, WhatsApp, and under them a plain typed in-page link "Yorumları oku". Columns 7–12: the box seen from the front: year divider tabs "2025" and "2023" and two card edges behind one large slip (Fiş 01/10, about 560px wide, rotated −1deg) carrying the Samsun review in Besley italic, the typed name, and a violet stamp "Müşterinin sözü · değiştirilmedi".

FIRST SCREEN (390×844): title, lead, the three buttons, then the box with slip 01, so the customer's words start on the first screen; the typed record and the "Yorumları oku" link follow the box.

LIST: the other nine slips are dealt in number order under year dividers: three to a row from 1240px, two to a row below that (an odd last slip centred), one on narrow screens. Year tabs use one lettering in the box and on the dividers (Courier Prime Bold, 22px; 20px narrow). The blank slip 11 carries the stamp box ("Damga yeri"); the dealer block carries the round seal.

FORM: structure 7 of 7 on the ordered list, the dealt lead. seed:231ef2769a774b5a

SIGNATURE: the lead slip rises out of the box once on arrival (transform only, content visible at rest); reduced motion: it stands raised.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
