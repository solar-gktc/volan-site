# Kalite Politikamız (kalite-politikamiz.html)

## Scope and mode
Draft of the quality-policy page, one of five corporate pages under Hakkımızda. Mode: Persuade. Turkish only. Inherits the Service Booklet world (DESIGN.md) unchanged. Not linked from the menu until approved.

## Audience, job, action
Post-warranty Volvo owner asking "is this as good as the dealer?". The page states the policy and shows how quality is kept on one car from acceptance to handover. Primary action: Randevu al (WhatsApp); Ara and Yol tarifi beside it.

## Proof and content
- Four policy clauses: live volan.com.tr/sayfa/kalite-politikamiz (read 2026-11-16).
- Station lines (teşhis, kayıt, teslim): live volan.com.tr homepage. "Kalite, Güven ve Süreklilik" list: live Hakkımızda page.
- User-pinned (tesisimiz.html): diagnosis is free; no repair without the customer's approval.
- Client's five-step order: Doğru teşhis, Teknik yetkinlik, Şeffaf süreç, Aracınıza özen, İşimizin arkasında durmak.
- The six stations are the page's own structure, not a sourced Volan process; the sheet is labelled "Örnek kayıt". Authored and awaiting Volan's confirmation (marked on the page with the bracketed typed note): the Kabul and Kontrol stations, the Onay sentence.
- Gaps shown as gaps, each once, in one style (form label, dotted fill-in line, bracketed typed note): the named standard behind "uluslararası standartlara uygun", warranty terms, certificates, acceptance and final-check lists, training record.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is the booklet's stamp page: one car's record with six control stamps, Kabul to Teslim. It refuses the framed policy statement over a row of value cards.

OWN-WORLD: Card stock, a plate-white record sheet with dashed stamp boxes, stamp-violet ink stamps at mixed angles, a navy leaf in the double foil frame for the policy text, typed record fields, blank fill-in lines for what is missing, stamp-box buttons, square corners.

STORY: The visitor sees six stamps and understands quality here is a sequence that is recorded. They read the four policy clauses (hanging numerals), then each station with the client's own sentence or a citation of its clause (Madde 3, Madde 4), carrying the same stamp, wording and angle as on the sheet. The two firm promises (free diagnosis, no work without approval) sit in the hero and in the Teşhis and Onay stamps. The section closes on the "Kalite, Güven ve Süreklilik" list beside the client's five-step order. Missing documents are visibly blank, so the rest is believed. They book.
