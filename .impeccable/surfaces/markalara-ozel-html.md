## Scope and mode

Draft page `markalara-ozel.html` (menu: Hakkımızda › Markalara Özel Prensibimiz). Mode: persuade. Visual world fixed by DESIGN.md (The Service Booklet); only the composition is new.

## Audience, job, action

A Volvo, Mercedes-Benz or Volkswagen owner in Ankara. Job: find their own brand, read how Volan treats it, book. Action: "Randevu al" (WhatsApp, message pre-filled with the brand), Ara, Yol tarifi.
Content: the client's three principle sentences (volan.com.tr/sayfa/markalara-ozel-hizmet-prensibimiz, read 2026-11-16, verbatim). Volvo depth claim limited to "uzun yıllar" (no years). Mercedes-Benz and Volkswagen get no lineage claim. No maker logos; brand names as text.

## Structures considered (ordered by resonance)

1. Three stacked record leaves, one per brand.
2. Index tabs on one open page.
3. Principles × brands table.
4. Key board: three key tags on a rail; the visitor picks their own tag.
5. One large Volvo leaf beside two small slips.
6. Blank acceptance slip the visitor fills in.
7. Depth ruler by year (rejected on truth: no years).

## Direction contract

THESIS: At a service you hand over your key and it gets a paper tag. The page is the key board: three tags, one per brand, and the visitor takes their own. What is written on each tag differs honestly (Volvo has a "Geçmiş" line, the others do not). It refuses three equal brand cards with logos.
OWN-WORLD: Navy board with the double foil frame; a foil rail with square pegs; plate-white paper key tags with two cut corners, a reinforced slot and a string, slightly rotated, plate shadow (square cuts only, no circle); ruled-form fields (Archivo labels, Courier values, dotted blanks for gaps); a 4px double navy record box for the principles; violet ticks and seal; stamp-box buttons.
STORY: The visitor sees three tags, picks their brand, reads that brand's record (what Volan can honestly say, with gaps shown), reads the three principles that apply to every brand, and books with a message that already names the brand.
FIRST VIEWPORT: (1440×900) One navy board inside the measure. Left six columns: H1 in light foil gold (display size), one lead sentence in stock, Randevu al / Ara stamp buttons. Right six columns: the rail with three hanging tags (Volvo, Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen), all three lower ends clearly overhanging the board onto the stock (about 40 to 100px); Volvo is selected (ticked, straightened, slid 10px down its string; the peg stays on the rail). Below the board the open record begins. Narrow: text, then the three tags stacked as horizontal tags on a vertical rail.
FORM: The key board, #4 of my seven grounded structures, dealt lead of the roll. seed:92f2d4bf3c47008e. Signature interaction: choosing a tag swings it once around its peg, ticks it, and opens that brand's record; ARIA tabs with arrow keys, Home, End. On the wide layout the page does not scroll on a choice; on narrow it scrolls to the record heading. A #kayit-… link opens and scrolls to that record. Each record links its "üç prensiple" sentence to the principles box (#prensip), which on narrow comes after the record. Without script all three records are listed and no tag is ticked.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
