# Tesisimiz (tesisimiz.html)

## Scope and mode
The facility page of the Volan Otomotiv site. Mode: Experience (the building leads). Turkish only. Inside the Service Booklet world (DESIGN.md); its own composition.

## Audience, job, action
Post-warranty Volvo owners (and Mercedes-Benz / VW owners) who want to see where their car will be handled. They should leave knowing what happens on each floor and that diagnosis is free and nothing is repaired without their approval; then book (WhatsApp), call, or get directions.

## Pinned by the user
- Real building photo plus the interactive 3D model (`../facility-modelling`, web copy via `tools/tesis-model.sh`, meshopt).
- Cards appear around the model when a unit is clicked; the model is interactive.
- Layout A "Cephe açılır" chosen from three sketches (`.impeccable/mocks/decision/tesis/`).
- Items, in this order: 1 Mekanik, 2 Elektrik Sistem Onarımları, 3 Arıza Tespit (points to the office; diagnosis is free; work goes ahead only with the customer's approval), 4 Oto Kuaför, 5 Kaporta / Boya (lower floor), 6 Ofis (experienced team), 7 Bekleme Salonu.
- Mekanik opens two cards of its own: Mekanik and Yıllık Bakım. Elektrik Sistem Onarımları has its own single card. Clicking either workshop on the model opens the Mekanik pair.
- A picked unit keeps the other floors on screen (spread apart and faded) and focuses on its own floor (Kaporta / Boya: the bottom floor).
- Scrolling walks through units 1 to 7, one per scroll step, before the page moves on to the contact section.
- Menu: Tesisimiz replaces Multimedya; Multimedya moves under Hakkımızda.
- The building model is still being updated; the web copy is regenerated by the script.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The real facade opens into the building. The visitor scrolls and the photograph hands over to the 3D model from the same side, which turns and lifts its floors apart; every unit is then a numbered pin, and its card is tipped in beside the model on a leader line. It refuses the hero-over-card-grid facility page and the floating 3D widget in a box.
OWN-WORLD: Flat card stock (#eef0f5, as site.css renders it; the 32px ruling in DESIGN.md is not drawn site-wide) under a transparent WebGL stage; navy cover panel with the double gold foil rule over the photo; paper-white plates with typed captions as cards; navy numbered pins and 1.5px navy leader lines; double-ruled stamp-box buttons; Besley, Courier Prime, Archivo Narrow. Square corners, plate shadow only on paper.
STORY: The visitor sees the real building and the title, scrolls, watches the facade become the model and open into three floors, picks a unit from the model or the numbered bar, reads its card (photo, where it is, what happens there), books from it, and ends on the dealer block.
FIRST VIEWPORT: (1440×900) Full-bleed facade photo under the menu; navy cover panel at lower left with H1 "Tesisimiz", one lead line, Binayı gez (primary) and Randevu al; the typed address plate at lower right. Narrow: photo on top, cover panel as a full-width block at the bottom of the screen.
FORM: Cephe açılır, #1 of the three sketches the user saw (A), chosen by the user over the roll. seed:f4b8c9eb12e2d8e3
SIGNATURE: Scroll-driven handover: the photo fades as the model's matching exterior view takes its place, the camera swings to the overview, walls drop away and the floors separate; then pins appear and the stage becomes interactive (drag to turn, pins and bar to select; selecting spreads the floors, fades the others, frames the unit's floor, and tips in its card(s) on a leader line). Further scrolling walks the units 1 to 7 in order, then returns to the whole building before the contact section. Reduced motion: no scroll scene; photo, then the interactive model in its overview pose. No WebGL: photo, a still render, and every card in a grid.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
