# Sürdürülebilirlik Yaklaşımımız (surdurulebilirlik.html)

## Scope and mode
Draft of the sustainability page, one of five corporate pages under Hakkımızda. Mode: Read. Turkish only. Inside the Service Booklet world (DESIGN.md); own composition. Not linked from the menu until approved.

## Audience, job, action
Post-warranty Volvo owners (and Mercedes-Benz / VW owners) who ask what happens to the oil, filters, batteries, tyres and paint waste that leave their car, and how the workshop uses energy. They should leave knowing what Volan states and which records will back it. Action: Randevu al (WhatsApp), Ara, Yol tarifi.

## Proof and content
- Client copy: the three sentences of volan.com.tr/sayfa/surdurulebilirlik-yaklasimimiz, read 2026-11-16, quoted whole.
- No numbers, disposal documents, licensed-firm names, certificates or photos exist. Every such slot is a labelled blank.
- The waste types in the register (atık yağ, filtre, akü, lastik, boya ve solvent) are a draft list for Volan to confirm.

## Structures considered (ordered by resonance)
1. Waste register table by waste type. 2. Three topic entries (Enerji / Atık / Kaynak), each with a stamp box. 3. Signed declaration with numbered attachments. 4. Route of a litre of used oil as a flow. 5. Evidence matrix: topics against record types, ticked or blank. 6. The slip of what leaves the car in a service, with a status record up front. 7. Visitor questions with answer lines.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is the booklet's record of what leaves the car in a service: the statement is typed in, and every line that needs a document is a blank fill-in line with an empty stamp box. It refuses the green "eco" hero with leaf icons and a row of three value cards.

OWN-WORLD: The Service Booklet unchanged: card stock, navy, stamp violet with the ink filter, plate-white paper objects with the plate shadow, Besley / Courier Prime / Archivo Narrow, double-ruled stamp-box buttons, square corners. Page parts: a status record card, a punched delivery slip with dotted fill-in lines, blank digit boxes, empty dashed plates with drawn corner mounts, labelled "Volan'dan gelecek", captions typed. Every proposed list (waste types, energy topics, measurement rows, attachments) carries a typed "taslaktır; Volan onaylayacak" note.

STORY: The visitor reads Volan's three sentences, sees at once that the statement is recorded and the documents are awaited, follows each waste type to its blank line, sees which figures and documents will be added, and reaches contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440×900): columns 1–7: H1 "Sürdürülebilirlik Yaklaşımımız" navy, about 62px; the client's closing sentence as an italic lead; one paragraph saying what the page holds. Columns 9–12: the status record card (plate, 0.8deg) with five rows, one ticked ("Beyan"), four blank, and the stamp "Beyan kayıtlı / Belgeler bekleniyor". Contact stays in the menu bar and the fab.

FORM: "What leaves the car" slip with a status record, #6 of 7. seed:b40102633f8cd074

SIGNATURE: One stamp presses into the status card when the card is in view (the "Beyan" tick presses in just before it); below 900px the stamp box sits above the list so it is in the first screen.
