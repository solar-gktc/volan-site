# Galeri (galeri.html)

## Scope and mode
Draft of the photo gallery, page name "Galeri" (renamed from Resimler; it holds photographs only). Mode: Experience; the photographs lead. Turkish only. Inherits the Service Booklet world (DESIGN.md) unchanged; only the composition is new. Draft for the user's approval, not linked from the menu.

## Audience, job, action
Post-warranty Volvo owners checking that the 1954 lineage is real. Job: look through all 21 client photos (12 archive scans, 9 building photos of 2026-10-04), open any one large with its caption. Contact (Randevu al, Ara, Yol tarifi) closes the page.

## Proof and content
- Images: only `photos/` (derivatives in `assets/img/galeri/`, archive scans at native size, never upscaled).
- Captions describe what is visible. Archive plates read "Arşivden, tarihsiz: …". No names of people, places or years.
- Gaps: no current photos of the team, the workshop interior or equipment in `photos/`; three blank panels labelled "Volan'dan gelecek: …".

## Direction contract
THESIS: The complete picture record as one fold-out (leporello) bound into the booklet: a single strip of 21 numbered plates pulled sideways out of a navy cover panel. It refuses the equal-thumbnail masonry grid and the hero-plus-cards gallery.

OWN-WORLD: Navy cover panel with the double gold foil frame and light-foil-gold title; the strip is paper panels joined at flat fold lines, archive panels on archive cream (one flat tone), today's panels on plate white with each photo tipped in as a plate (10px plate-white border, plate shadow), blank dashed panels for what is missing; the 1px strong-rule border draws each fold; typed plate numbers and captions (Courier Prime), Archivo Narrow labels, stamp-box pager buttons, square corners, the plate shadow under the strip and the tipped-in plates only.

STORY: The visitor sees the cover and the first archive prints at once, pulls the strip (scroll, swipe, arrow keys, pager) from the stone-walled workshop to today's facade, opens a plate on the navy viewing board, and finds the typed list of all plates (number and short name, no pixel sizes), the archive note and the contact block below. On narrow screens number, photo and caption sit as one group at the top of each panel.
