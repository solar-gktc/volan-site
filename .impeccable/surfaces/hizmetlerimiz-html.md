# Hizmetlerimiz (hizmetlerimiz.html)

## Scope and mode
The services page of the Volan Otomotiv site. Mode: Persuade. Turkish only. Its own composition inside the Service Booklet world (DESIGN.md); it does not reuse the homepage's 3D drive.

## Audience, job, action
Post-warranty Volvo owners (and Mercedes-Benz / VW owners) checking whether Volan can handle everything their car needs. They should leave believing "one place does it all" and book (Randevu al via WhatsApp, prefilled with the service), call, or message.

## Pinned by the user
- Slogan: "Aracınızın tüm ihtiyaçları için tek noktadan çözüm sunuyoruz."
- Only the 8 services on volan.com.tr/hizmetler: Bakım & Onarım, Yedek Parça, Araç Arıza Tespiti, Sigorta / Kasko Onarım İşlemleri, Elektronik Sistem Onarımları, Kaporta, Boya, Mekanik Onarım.
- Real Turkish copy drafted from the current site; staff counts, certificate, guarantee terms and insurer names appear as bracketed placeholders.
- Round 1 (Maintenance Schedule table + blueprint) was built and REJECTED: "too much unnecessary info takes space; the ticks mean nothing to the customer; we need a longer page that explains the services better."
- Layout: the Volan Wheel sketch (`.impeccable/mocks/decision/hizmetler-v2/volan.html`), locked on the decision page (round 2, no steer).
- Hero must be photo-rich (user: "we need more visuals in the hero part").
- Content: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/hizmetler-v2/icerik.md`. Per service only: lead, one paragraph, Ne zaman, Neler dahil, one action. The process is told once.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Volan means steering wheel. One navy ring carries the eight services on its rim, so "tek nokta" is a single wheel the visitor can see whole; the page then explains each service at length, one per screen, while a small wheel turns to the one being read. It refuses the row of service cards, the icon grid and the tick table.
OWN-WORLD: Card stock ground; the navy ring with a gold-foil double rule; round photo windows set in paper rims with the plate shadow; Besley titles and text, Courier Prime captions and record data, Archivo Narrow uppercase buttons; square corners elsewhere; double-ruled stamp-box buttons; violet 1954 seal on the dealer block. No eyebrows, no thin dividers, no gradients.
STORY: The visitor reads the slogan beside the wheel and sees all eight services as photos on its rim; picks one or scrolls; reads each service (photo, lead, paragraph, when to come, what is included) and books from it; reads how a visit works once, four short answers, then the dealer block.
FIRST VIEWPORT: (1440×900) Left: slogan as H1 (2 lines), one sub sentence, typed location line, Randevu al / Ara stamp buttons, then a tipped-in archive photo (the user asked for more visuals in the hero). Right: a giant navy ring cropped by the right edge, the building photo in its hub, eight round service photos on its rim with names, Bakım & Onarım largest at a gold pointer. Narrow: the whole wheel under the text (eight photos on the rim, building in the hub), then a two-column list of service names, so every photo has a readable name nearby.
FORM: The Volan Wheel, #2 of my seven grounded structures for round 2, dealt by the roll. seed:7e8edbbab7301867
SIGNATURE: A small sticky wheel beside the services turns so its gold pointer sits on the service being read, with that service's name in the hub; its rim dots and the hero's rim photos jump to each service. On narrow screens it becomes a sticky bar (small wheel + current name) that opens the list of eight. Reduced motion: the wheel jumps instead of turning.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
